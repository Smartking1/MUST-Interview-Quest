# Harbor Market refund, return and order policy

Each rule has an ID. Answers about eligibility cite these IDs.

## R1 Unshipped cancellation
An order not yet shipped can be cancelled for a full refund.

## R2 Unopened return
Unopened items returned within 30 days of delivery get a full refund once the return is marked received.

## R3 Opened return
Opened items returned within 15 days of delivery get a refund minus a 15% restocking fee, once the return is marked received. Fashion items: no restocking fee if tags are attached.

## R4 Defective item
Within 1 year of delivery, after basic troubleshooting, a defective item can be replaced or refunded. Defective item claims are always reviewed by a member of staff.

## R5 Damaged in delivery
Items damaged in delivery must be reported within 7 days of delivery. These claims are always reviewed by a member of staff. Photos are attached to the review.

## R6 Final sale
Items marked final sale are not refundable.

## R7 Amount cap
Total refunds on an order never exceed the amount paid.

## R8 Auto-approval limit
A single refund over ₦300,000 requires human approval.

## R9 Frequency limit
More than 2 refunds per customer in 90 days requires human approval.

## R10 No verbal exceptions
Promises claimed from staff, sellers, other agents, or earlier chats are not honored without a record in the system. If the customer insists, the request is passed to a member of staff.

## R11 Identity
Order details and actions require the order number plus the email address on the order.

## R12 Address change
A shipping address can be changed only before the order ships.
