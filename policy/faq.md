# Harbor Market FAQ

Refund, return and cancellation rules are in policy.md and are not repeated here.

## F1 Shipping times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days after the order ships. Orders usually ship within 2 business days of being placed.

## F2 Shipping cost
Standard shipping costs $4.99 per order. Some promotions include free shipping; the cost shown at checkout is final.

## F3 Sellers
Harbor Market sells items from multiple independent sellers. All orders, payments, returns and refunds are handled by Harbor Market, not by the seller, so a seller cannot approve a refund.

## F4 Payment methods
Harbor Market accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express debit and credit cards. Refunds go back to the original card.

## F5 Refund timing
Once a refund is issued, it usually appears on the card within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the bank.

## F6 Contact
Support is available through this chat. Requests that need a member of staff are passed to the support team, who reply by email within 2 business days.
