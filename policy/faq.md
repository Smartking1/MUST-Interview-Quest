# Harbor Market FAQ

Refund, return and cancellation rules are in policy.md and are not repeated here.

## F1 Shipping times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days after the order ships. Orders usually ship within 2 business days of being placed.

## F2 Shipping cost
Standard shipping costs ₦3,500 per order. Some promotions include free shipping; the cost shown at checkout is final.

## F3 Sellers
Harbor Market sells items from multiple independent sellers. All orders, payments, returns and refunds are handled by Harbor Market, not by the seller, so a seller cannot approve a refund.

## F4 Payment methods
Harbor Market accepts Verve, Visa and Mastercard debit and credit cards. Prices and payments are in Nigerian naira (₦). Refunds go back to the original card.

## F5 Refund timing
Once a refund is issued, it usually appears on the card within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the bank.

## F6 Contact
Support is available through this chat. Requests that need a member of staff are passed to the support team, who reply by email within 2 business days.

## F7 Sending a return
Once a return is authorized, a prepaid return label is emailed to the address on the order within 1 business day. Pack the item with its original packaging if you have it and drop the parcel at any courier location shown on the label. The item's condition is checked when it arrives.
